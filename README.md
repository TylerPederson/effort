# Effort
Explore and escape from an Eerie castle! Gather some weapons and equipment, and fight your way through corridors and rooms to pull levers to hopefully find a way to freedom. As you fight, you'll find powerups and stronger enemies - You will have to put in some 'effort' to escape!

# Features
*Quick Gameplay:* Each play through should take about 10-15 minutes

*Chance and Treasure:* Powerups become available each time you clear the enemies out of a room, but what powerups are available are different each time

*Risk and Reward:* Be careful not to die, or you'll have wasted all that effort. Death is permanent, and you'll have to try all over again.

*Retro Aesthetics:* The castle that you are trapped in is nearly liminal, but the fog, lighting, and pixelation will bring back memories of games from long ago.

# How to Play
*Controls:* WASD to move, E to interact, Tab for Inventory, Shift to sprint. Esc to pause and controls can be viewed from settings.

*Free Download*: Effort can be freely downloaded for Windows from itch: https://tylerpederson.itch.io/effort

*Extract and Play:* Just Download, Extract and run the game! You should put in effort in the game, not on the download!
